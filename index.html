Ghana

Ghana is a country in West Africa bordered by Togo, Burkina Faso, and Côte d'Ivoire, with the Gulf of Guinea to the south. March is Ghana Month, where we celebrate our rich culture through traditional foods like banku, fufu, and kokonte, and wear colorful kente attire.
